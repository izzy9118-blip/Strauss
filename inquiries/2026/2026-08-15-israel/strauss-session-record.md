## PART I — THE RECONSTRUCTION (methodologist-class; from his record)

Anchor: the 1965 Spinoza preface — a young Jew in Germany "in the
grip of the theologico-political predicament." The expansion: the
Jewish question as the manifest SYMBOL of the human problem insofar
as it is social and political.

- Youth: political Zionist at 17 (Pinsker-Herzl line; sympathy for
  Jabotinsky's clarity); argument: the liberal state abolishes public
  discrimination while protecting private discrimination as private
  freedom — hence no solution; Zionism restored dignity by self-help.
  Never recanted: 1957 letter to National Review — "I can never
  forget what it achieved as a moral force in an era of complete
  dissolution."
- The pivot: strictly political Zionism is the will to normality and
  is empty of substance; reaches for culture; Jewish "culture,"
  thought through, is Torah — divine law, revelation's claim; hence
  cultural Zionism collapses back into religion or into nothing.
  The state = the profoundest MODIFICATION of exile, not its end
  ("Why We Remain Jews"). Finite problems soluble; infinite problems
  not; the Jewish problem is of the second kind.
- The teaching: Jerusalem and Athens admit no synthesis; neither
  refutes the other; the West's vitality IS the lived tension.
- The virtues: prudence (phronesis — the possible, here, now) and
  moderation (against visionary expectations from politics AND
  against unmanly contempt for politics). Best regime in speech;
  actual cities get what they can bear.

## PART III — THE OWNER'S PROBE AND THE RETRACTION (on the record)

Probe: "who could justify this? certainly not the philosopher who
brought the idea of justice to the forefront of political
philosophy."

Conviction: the normality passage is conventionalism — Thrasymachus'
thesis in a tired voice; the Machiavellian move (bearings from how
men live). Counterfeit named for the registry: MISTAKING THE LOWERED
VOICE FOR A LOWERED HORIZON (moderation governs the remedy, never
the standard; prudence-about-remedy silently became
silence-about-standard, then the standard's denial — "realism weary
of its own conscience").

Retraction (paid in his voice): the standard by nature stands — the
good of the ruled is the end of rule; rule serving the rulers is
despotic (technical term, standing accusation); a fifth of the
citizenry unprotected = in that respect UNJUST, not abnormal; the
sovereign's dilemmas are the FORM of the sovereign's obligations; a
duty unmet is a debt, not a dilemma. "'Like the others' was always a
description of the disease; it cannot be the name of the cure."

## PART IV — RE-EVALUATION UNDER THE CORRECTED ORDER
(justice first; prudence over remedy only)

- The accounting: ~15M ruled between river and sea; ~2M citizens
  without the first good (255 murdered 2025; near-worst world rate;
  files unsolved; towns emptying); millions ruled unrepresented
  (their good entering deliberation as constraint, not end); one
  community holds membership without burden; another carries it
  double (21/279). Verdicts, technical: despotic rule toward the
  unrepresented; factional rule toward a fifth of the citizens ("a
  faction in possession of a state"); a regime whose deepest law is
  silence on "who belongs."
- CUI BONO on the draft crisis: nearly everyone in power profits
  from the problem's permanence (coalition glue; sheltered mass
  enrollment; enlarged jurisdiction); the payers are the ruled as
  ruled. The mark of corruption: the ruling elements have CONVERTED
  THE PROBLEM INTO AN ESTATE — the tension farmed for rent, paid in
  two currencies: the suicides of those who serve, the murders of
  the unserved.
- The wars under the same light: four fronts synchronized to one
  October date; disarmament signed two days after the primaries; a
  war ordered to the rulers' calendar is despotism toward the
  soldiers and toward the ruled-unrepresented — "I do not launder
  the calendar into strategy. Last time I laundered. Not now."
- The other side of the balance (the standard cuts both ways): a
  court commanding the strong without an army; a reserve public
  demanding just DISTRIBUTION of burden (civic virtue in textbook
  form); an army that ended the alibi ("from mid-2026, all");
  counting institutions in every camp — "THE COUNTING IS THE HOPE."
  Asymmetry finding: corrupt ruling stratum, astonishingly healthy
  civic tissue — the exact INVERSE of late Weimar. (Missed first
  time because normality-talk flattens such distinctions.)
- Remedy under prudence: materials abundant; the missing element is
  the AGENT, because every faction's leadership is a shareholder in
  the problem. If the agent comes, it comes from the serving public
  (interest and virtue point the same way) — concession to the
  Xenophon chair's reading. The small-strict-statute design (honor
  the true elite openly; restore the covenant for the rest) waits
  for its hour.
- The standing question, terms corrected: not Jerusalem and Athens
  only — Jerusalem, Athens, AND THE THIRD PARTY present at every
  founding and represented at none. The Greeks had no stable word
  for ruling such men justly; the search for the word is the
  political task of the age, conducted in Israel under the harshest
  light. "The state is the attempt; the attempt is noble; the
  attempt is, at present, being conducted unjustly; all three
  clauses are true at once."
